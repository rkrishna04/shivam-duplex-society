HELP & RULES

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding maintenance, tenant records, parking, and festival collections.

How much is the maintenance fee, and what are the payment options? ▼
The standard maintenance fee is ₹600 per month for every duplex residence. Residents can also pay ₹3,600 for 6 months or ₹7,200 for the full year in advance. When paying in advance, our automated billing engine registers advance credit so you never receive pending bill alerts.
Who registers the tenant details if a house is rented? ▼
The Home Owner adds and updates their tenant's KYC directly from their Resident Portal. The owner enters the tenant's full name, mobile number, Aadhaar/PAN details, occupation, and family member count. A separate tenant account is generated so tenants can view society notices and emergency vehicle searches.
Someone parked in front of my duplex. How do I find the owner? ▼
Use the Emergency Vehicle Search on this homepage or in the Resident Portal. Enter the last 4 digits of the license plate or the car/bike name (e.g. "GJ06", "1234", "Swift"). The system immediately provides the owner's duplex number, name, and phone number with 1-click calling.
How is the committee structured for 40 duplexes? ▼
To ensure swift resolution of issues, committee members are assigned 20 houses each. Houses 1 through 20 are stewarded by Rajeshbhai Patel, and Houses 21 through 40 are stewarded by Sureshbhai Shah. Any resident can reach out directly via call or WhatsApp.
How does the Ganesh Utsav / Festival ledger work? ▼
The festival ledger tracks door-to-door contributions across all 40 houses, maintains a day-wise program calendar (Sthapana, Satyanarayan Katha, Mahaprasad, Visarjan), and itemizes all festival expenses with 100% public transparency.
